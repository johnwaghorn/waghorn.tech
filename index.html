About

We're a small team, and that's the point.

Waghorn Technology is a UK software consultancy made up of experienced developers who'd rather do a few projects properly than many at once. Most of our work comes from people we've worked with before.

We like problems where the technology is in service of something concrete: a process that's eating someone's week, a system that's grown past what it was designed for, a product that needs to exist before the next funding conversation.

If that sounds like your situation, we're easy to talk to and quick to tell you if we're not the right fit.

How we work
01 - Talk
A call, no charge. You describe the problem, we say whether we can help.
02 - Scope
A short written proposal: what we'll build, how long, what it costs.
03 - Build
Weekly demos in your repo. You see progress, not status reports.
04 - Hand over
Documentation, a walkthrough, and support for as long as you need it.
